02 / About

About Me

Technology has always felt like the most direct way to turn an idea into something real, and that curiosity is what first pulled me toward web development. Building interfaces that people can actually use, and watching a project go from a blank file to a working product, is what keeps me hooked on this field.

Alongside the web development side, I've become genuinely interested in artificial intelligence and how it can be woven into everyday applications to make them smarter and more useful. That interest is shaping the direction of my Software Engineering journey: I want to build applications where thoughtful engineering and practical AI work together.

I see learning as an ongoing habit rather than a phase. Every project I take on, whether it's a coursework assignment or something built purely out of curiosity, is a chance to pick up a new tool, refine my code, or understand a concept a little more deeply than before.
